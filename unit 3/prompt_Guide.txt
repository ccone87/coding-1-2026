1. My game is an open world crafting and survival game where 
are able to collect resources to craft and built weapon, food, shelter, and even potions. 
The challenges you will face in the game are fighting off animals and beast that each have different hunting strategy.

2. The game will have welcome home screen where you start a new world or continue making progress in your previous worlds.

3. Swords, knifes, guns, food, and potions are objects that will be in the game. 

4. The player will use W,A,S,D to move around, the mouse to look around, left click select items,and right click to open doors.

5. The enemy with attack you once get within close. Different Enemies or creatures will attack shooting from a far, 
using a weapon to kill, or trying to eat you.

6. If you die in the game you will loose all of your inventery and will respawn in a ramdom spot.
